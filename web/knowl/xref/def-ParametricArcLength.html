Definition 10.3.8. Parametric Arc Length.

If the functions \(x(t)\) and \(y(t)\) are continuous on \([a,b]\) and differentiable on \((a,b)\text{,}\) the the length of the parametric curve
\begin{align*} x \amp = x(t)\\ y \amp = y(t) \end{align*}
on \(a\leq t\leq b\) is
\begin{equation*} \ell = \int_{t=a}^{t=b} \sqrt{(x'(t))^2+(y'(t))^2}\;dt\text{.} \end{equation*}
