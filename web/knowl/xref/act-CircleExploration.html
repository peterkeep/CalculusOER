Activity 10.1.6. Explore Some Curves.

We’ll start off with a family of parametric curves, where we can change the functions and hopefully predict some of the curve’s behavior.
🔗

(a)

Let’s start by thinking about the parametric curve:
\begin{align*} x \amp = \cos(t) \\ y \amp = \sin(t) \end{align*}
for \(0\leq t \leq 2\pi\text{.}\)
🔗
Before you visualize any of this, think about the behavior of the \(x\)-values and \(y\)-values. What do you think they’ll do? What will this curve look like?
🔗
Visualize the movement of the \(x\)-values, the \(y\)-values, and the complete parametric curve using the graphing tool below.
