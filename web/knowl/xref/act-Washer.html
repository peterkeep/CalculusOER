(c)

Use the slice-and-sum process to create an integral expression representing the volume of this solid.
🔗
Solution.
\begin{align*} V_k \amp= \left(\pi \left(f(x_k^*)\right)^2 - \pi\left(g(x_k^*)\right)\right)\Delta x \\ \amp = \pi\left((f(x_k^*))^2-(g(x_k^*))^2\right)\Delta x\\ V \amp \approx \sum_{k=1}^n \pi\left((f(x_k^*))^2-(g(x_k^*))^2\right)\Delta x\\ V \amp = \lim_{n\to\infty} \sum_{k=1}^n \pi\left((f(x_k^*))^2-(g(x_k^*))^2\right)\Delta x\\ \amp = \int_{x=a}^{x=b} \pi\left((f(x))^2-(g(x))^2\right)\;dx \end{align*}
