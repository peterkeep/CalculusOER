Theorem 10.2.9. Polar/Cartesian Coordinate Conversion.

For a point \((r,\theta)\) in the polar coordinate system, the corresponding Cartesian point \((x,y)\) can be written where:
\begin{align*} x \amp = r\cos(\theta)\\ y \amp = r\sin(\theta) \end{align*}
🔗
For a point \((x,y)\) in the Cartesian coordinate system, a corresponding polar point \((r,\theta)\) can be written where:
\begin{align*} \tan(\theta) \amp = \frac{y}{x}\\ r^2 \amp = x^2 + y^2 \end{align*}
